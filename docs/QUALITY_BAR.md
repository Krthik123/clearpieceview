# Quality bar

A video ships only when every line here is true. If one fails, fix the video or do not publish it.

## Packaging

- [ ] The title makes a specific promise in plain words, and reads fully on a phone.
- [ ] The thumbnail is understood in one second at small size, with one focal point and little or no text.
- [ ] Title and thumbnail say different things that add up to one idea.
- [ ] The video delivers exactly what the title and thumbnail promise.

## First 30 seconds

- [ ] The first sentence confirms the viewer clicked the right video.
- [ ] No intro animation, no "welcome back", no asking for a subscribe before giving anything.
- [ ] The viewer knows what they will get and why it is worth staying for.

## Body

- [ ] Every section earns its place. If cutting it loses nothing, it is cut.
- [ ] Something changes on screen often enough that a viewer never waits: a cut, a visual, a new point.
- [ ] Each point is shown, not only said, wherever it can be.
- [ ] The best material is not saved for the end.
- [ ] No dead air, no repeated points, no filler recap.

## Ending

- [ ] The video ends when the promise is delivered.
- [ ] One clear next step: one video to watch next, or one link. Not five.

## Sound and picture

- [ ] Voice is clear and level on phone speakers and on headphones.
- [ ] Music never competes with the voice.
- [ ] Text on screen is readable on a phone.
- [ ] Captions are accurate.

## Truth and rights

- [ ] Every factual claim has a source in the brief.
- [ ] All footage, music, and images are owned or licensed, with the license noted.
- [ ] Sponsorships, affiliate links, and free products are disclosed, in the video and in the description.
- [ ] Realistic AI-generated or altered footage is disclosed using the platform's own setting.

## The last test

- [ ] Watched start to finish, at normal speed, on a phone, without touching it. Any moment you wanted to skip gets cut.
