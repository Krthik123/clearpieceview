# Monetization

This file covers the streams a channel can run. For every way people earn from video, including selling video as a service, see `VIDEO_BUSINESS_MODELS.md`.

## Revenue streams, in the order they can start

| # | Stream | Can start | Depends on | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Affiliate links | First video | Viewers who buy; products worth recommending | Not started |
| 2 | Client or freelance work from the channel | First video | Videos that show a skill people pay for | Not started |
| 3 | Sponsorships | A consistent audience in a niche sponsors care about | Proof of views and who watches | Not started |
| 4 | Own product (digital product, course, template, community) | Once viewers ask for the same thing repeatedly | Trust, and an email list | Not started |
| 5 | Platform ad revenue | After the platform's partner program accepts the channel | Meeting the current thresholds | Not started |

Ad revenue is last on purpose. It needs the most audience and usually pays the least per viewer. Plan for streams 1 to 4 to carry the business.

## Rules

- Every brief names its money path before recording. "None, this one is for reach" is a valid answer, but it must be chosen.
- Own the audience. Send viewers to an email list from the start; a platform can change its rules or remove a channel.
- Recommend only what we would use. One bad recommendation costs more trust than it earns.
- Disclose every paid relationship, every time.
