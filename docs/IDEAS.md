# Ideas

Add ideas the moment they appear. Score them before picking one. Highest total goes next.

## Scoring (1 to 5 each)

- **Demand:** proof people watch this. A 5 has similar videos far outperforming the channels that posted them.
- **Edge:** why ours would be the best one on the topic.
- **Money:** how directly it can earn (a product to link, a sponsor fit, a lead for our own offer).
- **Ease:** 5 is makeable this week with what we have; 1 needs travel, gear, or weeks.

Write the idea as a title, not a topic. "Budget cameras" is a topic. A title is something you could put on the video today.
