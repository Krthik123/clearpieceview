## Picking the niche

A niche is worth entering when all three hold:

1. **Demand.** People already watch this. Proof is existing videos from small channels with far more views than the channel has subscribers.
2. **Edge.** We can make it better or different: real experience, access, data, a format nobody else uses, or simply more effort per video.
3. **Money.** Viewers in this niche buy things. There are products to recommend, sponsors who already pay creators here, or something we can sell.

Two out of three is a hobby. Write the evidence for each in this file before committing to a niche.

## Positioning

Fill in once the niche is set.

- **Viewer:** who exactly, and what they are trying to do.
- **Promise:** what they get from every video.
- **Why us:** the edge, in one sentence.
- **Not for:** who we are willing to lose.

## How we win

- **One video is the unit.** Each one must be worth clicking and worth finishing on its own. A channel is the sum of those.
- **Packaging before production.** If the title and thumbnail are not strong, the video does not get made.
- **Fewer, better.** A video that clears the quality bar beats three that do not.
- **Learn from outliers.** When a video beats the channel baseline, make the next three closer to it. See `METRICS.md`.
- **Money from day one.** Every video has a planned way to earn before it is recorded, even if that is a single affiliate link.

## What we do not do

- Mass-produced, templated, or re-uploaded content. Platforms demonetize it and viewers leave.
- Titles or thumbnails that promise something the video does not deliver.
- Recommending a product we would not use for the commission.
