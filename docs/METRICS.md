# Metrics

All numbers come from the platform dashboard. Do not estimate.

## What we watch

| Metric | Tells us | If it is low |
|---|---|---|
| Impressions click-through rate | Whether the title and thumbnail work | Change the packaging, not the video |
| Retention at 30 seconds | Whether the hook works | Rewrite the opening |
| Average percentage viewed | Whether the body holds attention | Cut harder; find where the graph drops |
| Views against channel baseline | Whether this idea is an outlier | Baseline is the median of the last 10 videos |
| Subscribers per 1,000 views | Whether viewers want more of this | The video did not show what the channel is |
| Revenue per video | Whether attention turns into money | Revisit the money path in the brief |

Views alone decide nothing. A video with fewer views and more revenue is the better video.

## Acting on the numbers

- A video well above baseline: make the next ones closer to it in topic, format, or packaging.
- Good click-through, poor retention: the video did not deliver the promise. Fix the content.
- Poor click-through, good retention: a good video nobody clicked. Change the title and thumbnail, then watch for a week.
- Both poor: wrong idea. Note why in `IDEAS.md` and move on.
